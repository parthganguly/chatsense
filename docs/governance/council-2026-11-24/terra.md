**CHATSENSE-TERRA-24**

**Authority gate: PASS.** The charter preserves the owner’s authority over scope, paid experiments, merges, releases and consequential privacy changes. It gives Opus a technical-feasibility decision without letting that resolve scientific objections, and it prevents council consensus from clearing Terra’s reproducible FAIL or evidence-based BLOCKED.

Neon’s absence does **not** stop routine authorized work: the routing protocol permits independent work to continue and factual tracking of receipts, state and blockers. Neon’s role-specific gate remains pending. Consequential publication requiring Neon must wait for explicit review of the actual text; a missing chair has no implied approval.

**Evidence gate: FAIL as a complete operating policy.** The charter requires independent verification and records invocation metadata, but it does not require enough provenance to substantiate every claim of review independence or economic attribution. This is a policy gap, not a finding that any particular run or expense was mishandled.

Concrete counterexamples:

- **False independence:** Sol implements a candidate, then gives a nominally fresh reviewer Sol’s diagnosis and expected fix. The reviewer agrees without finding an independent failure mode. A new run ID and prompt hash establish an invocation, not an independent judgment. The first-pass protocol requires prior reasoning and expected answers to be excluded; inherited reasoning must be labeled non-blind. Execution rule 3 also requires independent verification.
- **False economic attribution:** A receipt reports usage and a “known charge” of zero because the harness showed no charge, while a shared subscription or pooled provider bill carried the cost. Execution rule 2 asks for “any known charge,” which does not establish the payer, billing period or whether cost was actually measured. Zero observed charge is not evidence of zero economic cost.
- **Unsound acceptance:** A corrected candidate passes a smoke test, but the failed privacy invariant is never retested against the exact revision. Execution rules 3–4 require independent verification of repairs, an exact result and a revision history. A successful smoke or council agreement cannot substitute for that evidence.

Minimum revisions: require review receipts to identify the candidate revision, reviewer and relevant prior context, and state whether the verification was blind or otherwise independent. Require cost claims to identify their billing source and attribution basis; mark unavailable or shared costs as unknown rather than zero. Keep acceptance pending when required evidence is unavailable.

This verdict concerns the **operating policy only**. The S3 boundary records prior offline acceptance as an input; this review neither reopens nor confirms S3 implementation acceptance.
