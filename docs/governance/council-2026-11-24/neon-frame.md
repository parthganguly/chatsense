**Decision Frame**
Issue #38 authorizes only offline implementation and freeze of a 16-call descriptive pilot (Jev and GLiDE, 8 calls each; cards A/D/E; paired PNG and exact text; four byte-identical A repeats; no observations yet). We cannot edit the contract, run calls, merge, or change the product. The decision is purely evidentiary: setting interpretive boundaries before data exists.

**Human Problem Challenge**
Relationship cards help people grasp observable patterns and limits. Real comprehension fails under emotional stakes, selective reading, and interpersonal ambiguity—not merely visual-versus-text rendering or model variance. Using two synthetic readers risks mistaking model artifacts for human confusion, or missing how real users misread cards altogether.

**Neutral Decision Question**
What can this frozen 16-call pilot establish about card comprehension, and which outcomes, if any, would justify a product change rather than signaling synthetic-reader limits?
