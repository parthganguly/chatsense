## Decision

Keep S3 v2 available as a bounded synthetic-reader diagnostic. The experiment can characterize how two services answer particular prompted interpretations of three fixed cards under a fixed schedule. Even unanimous reference answers or repeatable model errors cannot establish human comprehension, prevalence, harm, the benefit of an edit, or release readiness. A validated and specific anomaly can justify offline inspection or a hypothesis for later testing. Product changes require separate evidence matched to the claim and the owner's authorization.

The human question is whether a person can use the card's historical evidence without turning it into a prediction or an explanation of the relationship. A person can correctly read the card and still come away unsure what to do. An answer picked under forced choice cannot distinguish a belief about the future from a decision about who should initiate contact.
