# ChatSense empirical construct map

Research proposal, 2026-10-10; issue [#24](https://github.com/parthganguly/chatsense/issues/24). No production authorization. Read with the [evidence review](psychometrics-evidence-review.md), [protocol](validation-protocol.md), and [decision](personal-model-architecture-decision.md). Evidence IDs refer to the review.

**A means a reproducible observation, not a validated psychological interpretation. B means a testable hypothesis, not a feature ready to ship. C means an unsupported or inappropriate product claim.** A single feature can have an A measurement and a B or C interpretation. More messages improve measurement of the selected record; they do not turn one person into many independent people.

## A — Observations supported by the available data

Definitions below are pinned to `main` at `2415a51385f60ff9e43ca2e26b66c8c9a7fd6267`, especially [behavioral contract](../../contracts/behavioral_contract.json), [relationship dynamics](../relationship-dynamics.md), and `packages/chatsense-core/src/relationship-dynamics.ts`. These are operational definitions, not natural psychological boundaries.

| Candidate | Reproducible measurement and denominator | What may be said; principal limitations |
|---|---|---|
| Volume, turns, participation | Non-system parsed messages per sender / all such messages; turns start at first valid message, sender change, or gap ≥360 minutes. Turn share = sender turns / all turns. Word counts are whitespace tokens. | “In this export…” Counts describe the parser's record; media placeholders, missing lines and burst typing affect interpretation. Equal volume does not establish reciprocity of care. |
| Initiation | Sender thread starts / all thread starts; first observed message and gaps ≥360 minutes define starts. Show integer numerator and denominator. | “You started 7 of 10 observed threads.” The first export boundary is left-truncated: its actual beginning is unknown. Sensitivity analysis must exclude that start. Starting first may reflect schedules, duties or the partner. |
| Reconnection | Sender first messages after gaps ≥1,440 minutes / all completed gaps meeting that rule. | Counts of recorded restarts, not reconciliation or desire to reconnect. Missing calls/other channels cannot be treated as no contact. |
| Follow-ups | Same sender sends again after ≥15 and <360 minutes before another sender responds. Separate event count from rate: distinct relevant turns containing a follow-up / relevant turns. | A turn can contain several follow-ups. The summary exposes event count, relevant-turn count and rounded rate, **not the exact rate numerator**; never reconstruct it from that rate. Exclude open final turns per existing code. No anxiety label. |
| Observed reply timing | Median minutes between adjacent different-sender messages, attributed to the later sender, plus count and distribution where available. | Sender-switch delay is conditional on an observed switch. It does not establish that the prior message requested a reply, when it was read, or that unanswered messages were rejected. Not the forecasting opportunity definition. |
| Pause / quiet so far | Completed adjacent-message gap distribution; current displayed quiet = supplied clock minus last exported message, with staleness caveat, never included as a completed gap. | “Quiet in this export” is not real-world silence. Historical restart fractions conditional on completed pauses are not future restart probabilities. |
| Rhythm | Counts by interpreted hour/day; activity = messages / active days, with coverage and active-day count separately shown. | Source offsets are absent; host timezone cannot recover them. Active-day rate hides inactive days; any future calendar-day rate needs genuinely covered days, not an assumed complete export. No sleep, mood or conscientiousness claim. |
| Longitudinal change | Same dyad/sender under contract windows (7/14/30 days) and existing metric-specific eligibility; before/after values and counts. | Threshold crossing is a descriptive rule, not statistical significance or a cause. Partial windows, seasonality and changed obligations may account for it. |
| Selected-export evidence rows | Existing metrics computed separately; explicitly declared self and conversation identity, per-row coverage, unknown timezone, duplicate exclusions and states. | Scientifically defensible as an optional descriptive view after separate design/privacy/comprehension gates. PR #27 is internal and unmerged; #28 stays deferred. No averaging of medians, confidence labels, or rows into a person score. |
| Cross-medium activity record | Only platform-native actions with verified definitions and their own denominators, if a future adapter is approved. | A WhatsApp reply, Instagram like and email are different opportunities. No cross-medium feature is available or validated by today's WhatsApp parser. |

Existing numerical floors are reply samples ≥5 per sender/period, starts ≥3, reconnections ≥2, relevant follow-up turns ≥3; eligible windows need ≥20 messages and ≥2 active days. Zero opportunities are unavailable, positive counts below floors insufficient, genuine zero numerators preserved. These engineering floors **do not establish psychometric reliability**. Existing “Strong/Useful/Light read” labels are copy tied to rules, not probabilities or independent evidence weights.

## B — Hypotheses requiring independent validation

All B rows require participant-held-out evaluation, later-time evaluation, uncertainty, independent criteria and improvement over the strongest simple baseline. The [protocol](validation-protocol.md) defines proposed success margins and the difference between failed, inconclusive and successful experiments. No row currently passes these gates for ChatSense.

| ID / construct, precisely scoped | Measurements worth testing | Alternatives and independent criteria | Falsification, held-out success and abstention |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1: A person's communication habits generalize to another **selected** dyad | Prior starts, completed restart shares, conditional latency and within-person dispersion, separately by dyad/time | Stable job, routine, partner demands, export selection, duplicate data. Criterion: later observed metadata from an unseen consented dyad; no personality instrument can define behavioral stability. S5–S6 motivate, not validate. | Fail the useful-generalization hypothesis if other-dyad history gives no practically meaningful improvement beyond context and simple history baselines. Success is H1 on untouched data. Abstain from a person-wide tendency if too few distinct dyads, incomplete coverage or contradictory effects. Stable habits alone are not traits. |
| B2: Extraversion / sociability is associated with contact behavior | Initiation, activity and breadth **only among enumerated consenting relationships**, temporal variability; never infer full network size from a few exports | Role, availability, age, selection of salient partners, overall phone use, partner behavior. Independent criterion: optional full BFI-2 Extraversion; Sociability facet exploratory, plus separately consented peer criterion if available. S1–S4, S7–S10. | H2: locked metadata model must improve over context-only prediction of an independently collected questionnaire and replicate in a new sample. A practically equivalent-to-zero increment falsifies this feature set's usefulness; it does not disprove personality. Abstain outside validated population/language/coverage. |
| B3: Agency and communion in interpersonal style | Starts and turn allocation as weak candidate agency indicators; contingent contact maintenance as a weak communion indicator | Assigned organizer role can produce high initiation; warmth can be expressed offline with sparse messages. Independent IPIP-IPC agency/communion or CSIV **values**, keeping traits and values distinct (S11). No message-count definition of “warmth.” | Require convergent **and discriminant** associations, incremental prediction beyond BFI-2/context, and unseen-dyad replication. Reject the proxy if it tracks work duties better than its intended criterion. Currently lower priority than B1–B2; abstain without instruments and diversified dyads. |
| B4: Conscientiousness / organization relates to temporal regularity | Repeatability of local-time distributions or timing variation, only with usable timezone provenance | Shift work, religious routine, school schedules, disability/accessibility needs, notification rules and automation. Independent BFI-2 Conscientiousness/Organization; self-reported schedules entered before results. S24 supports rhythm persistence, not this trait interpretation. | Falsify the personality interpretation if schedule-only models perform equivalently or transfer fails across schedule changes. Must improve independent-criterion prediction on unseen people and remain calibrated. No timezone or no varied schedules → abstain. |
| B5: A person's felt closeness is associated with communication patterns in that relationship | Within-dyad changes in initiation, observed latency and maintenance | Partner responsiveness, practical coordination, conflict, time apart, preference for calls. Criterion: independently completed Inclusion of Other in the Self (IOS) measure; optionally separate partner reports, never one person's rating as the other's feelings. S5, S12; IOS source S19. | Prospective within-person association must outperform prior closeness/context, not merely separate close and distant people. No increment or contrary replicated effect rejects the proxy. Even success authorizes at most a self-reported association, never an inferred third-party feeling or “relationship health.” |
| B6: A tendency transfers across media | Matched-period, matched-person and preferably matched-dyad measurements with platform-specific event definitions | Platform affordances, audience and relationship sorting, recommendation exposure, different devices and availability. Criteria: held-out platform behavior and independent BFI-2; same latent label cannot simply be assumed. S6, S13. | Train on one platform; evaluate without target-platform tuning on untouched people and on same-person paired data as separate tasks. Benefit must exceed platform/context baselines and measurement invariance must be defensible. Abstain for unmatched actions, identity uncertainty, absent platform or non-overlapping periods. |
| B7: State variation accompanies contextual changes | Person-centered deviations in timing/activity across observed periods | Measurement noise, missing data, partner change, calendar events, changing exports. Independent momentary self-reports using a reviewed state instrument; voluntary context diary, separate from app output (S7–S8). | Require lagged within-person prediction beyond prior state/context, distinguish it from between-person association, replicate across time. Defer until burden and instrument are selected in a new preregistration. Never call an unmeasured latent state depression, anxiety or avoidance. |

These candidates are deliberately asymmetric: S3's smartphone results make extraversion a stronger starting hypothesis than metadata-based openness or negative emotionality, but do not set expected ChatSense performance. Email and Instagram may express useful behavior; this review found no direct validation of the proposed selected-export feature set across those media.

## C — Unsupported or inappropriate outputs

| Proposed inference | Decision and reason |
|---|---|
| “You are an extrovert” from current WhatsApp exports; a definitive Big Five profile | Unsupported now. Continuous associations in other populations cannot justify an individual type or calibrated score here. Keep B2 as research only. |
| Slow replies mean avoidance; starts mean affection; frequent contact means health | Reject causal/emotional mapping. Availability, roles, partner effects and omitted channels are sufficient counterexamples. |
| Attachment style, mental disorder, distress or personality diagnosis | Reject product inference from these data. A questionnaire facet name is not a diagnosis. Clinical digital-phenotyping work is not validation of ChatSense. |
| Compatibility, quality, trustworthiness, “true self,” red flags, ghosting score | Reject composite/essentialist verdicts without an independently specified and defensible target; prohibited by existing product contract. |
| Psychological profile of a partner, employee or other nonconsenting person | Reject. Consent to contribute one's own data does not grant permission to profile others. |
| “70% initiation across these chats” without a declared aggregation rule | Reject as currently proposed output. Per-chat average, pooled event fraction and person-level estimate answer different questions. A hypothetical 70% example needs selected-export scope, exact counts, deduplication and weighting; today's approved slice has no pooling. |
| Independence-based confidence from more exports or correlated signals | Reject. The same person, dyad and episode can appear repeatedly; agreement among starts, restarts and volume is not three independent replications. |
| No further contact / future reply from export ending or historical restarts | Reject. Censoring and selection of completed pauses prevent this interpretation; forecasting remains blocked. |
| Cross-platform or culture-wide norms without validation | Defer collection/analysis; reject shipping equivalence or normality claims. BFI-2 validation itself is population/version-specific (S9–S10, S18). |

The map separates what the record contains, what could be learned with an appropriate study, and what the product is allowed to say. A promising association does not silently repeal a privacy or safety boundary.
