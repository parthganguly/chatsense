# Psychometrics, digital traces and the limits of ChatSense

Source-checked research synthesis, 2026-10-10. Proposal for [issue #24](https://github.com/parthganguly/chatsense/issues/24), not implementation or product validation. Companion documents: [construct map](construct-map.md), [validation protocol](validation-protocol.md), [architecture decision](personal-model-architecture-decision.md).

**2026-10-11 update:** the bounded [behavioural-transfer addendum](behavioural-transfer-evidence-addendum.md) adds S25–S29, including direct WhatsApp/Instagram timing evidence and an independent source/code audit. It supersedes any reading of this original review as finding no direct messaging-metadata studies; exact held-out behavioral transfer and personality interpretation remain unvalidated.

## Finding

**Digital behavior can contain information about questionnaire-measured personality, but the reviewed evidence does not validate personality assessment from ChatSense's selected WhatsApp exports.** The appropriate next question is whether narrow communication habits generalize to another relationship after accounting for context and selection. A favorable result would support a bounded behavioral model before it supported a psychological interpretation.

The three levels must remain separate:

1. **Relationship-specific:** timing and initiation are properties of an interaction involving two people, opportunities and constraints. The same person may behave differently with different partners.
2. **Medium-specific:** a platform changes available actions, audiences and exposure. A click, public post, email and private reply do not have interchangeable denominators.
3. **Cross-context:** some regularities persist across time, partners and channels. Persistence is evidence worth investigating, not by itself evidence that a latent trait caused the behavior.

McLuhan supplies a conceptual question about the medium's role, not a validation result. Conversely, contextual variability does not prove personality inference impossible. The useful scientific position is a falsifiable comparison of person, dyad, platform and environmental explanations.

## Scope, search and verification

This is a **targeted critical review**, not an exhaustive systematic review or a new meta-analysis. Searches on 2026-10-10 used the available web search/open tools, publisher articles, PubMed/PMC, author-hosted manuscripts and official instrument pages. Seed sources came from #24; backward/forward searches prioritized meta-analyses, original validation, longitudinal observations and held-out evaluations. Search families actually used included “personality prediction digital traces systematic review 2024 2025 cross platform metadata”, “Predicting Big Five personality traits from smartphone data”, “BFI-2 Soto John permission”, “Fleeson Gallagher fifteen experience sampling”, “multichannel social signatures”, “fast response times signal social connection”, and “social media feed algorithms attitudes behavior”. Recent searching located the 2024 synthesis and the revised August 2026 OECD measurement report.

Include sources for their actual construct/input/design, not their title's promise. Clinical/sensor reviews inform methodological limitations only; they do not justify diagnoses. LLM-generated personality labels, apparent-personality labels without an independent criterion, commercial accuracy claims and secondary summaries were not used as quantitative validation. This search cannot establish that no relevant paper exists. No pooled person count was fabricated from overlapping datasets.

Verification notation: **F** = relevant original full-text methods/results inspected by lead or Sol; **A** = original abstract/record inspected, with missing details explicitly withheld; **M** = methodological or instrument-permission source, not an empirical performance estimate. All entries checked 2026-10-10. “Not extracted” means the field was not verified in this review; it must not be silently filled from memory. An effect's CI describes its estimand, not an individual's psychological certainty. No paper's raw data or model was reanalyzed.

## Interpretation and competing hypotheses

**What supports investigation:** multiple digital-trace literatures find convergence with independent questionnaires, one rich smartphone study has nested evaluation without recording conversation content, and communication-allocation studies show persistence. Controlled observer research also contradicts the assertion that behavioral stability is necessarily an artifact of self-report or repeated environments. This justifies research, not a personal score.

**What weakens deployment:** selected best associations, reused datasets, some unvalidated models, reliability corrections, feature/criterion heterogeneity, and weak external transport. A pooled association is not a calibrated individual estimate; squaring r does not produce out-of-sample R² for an arbitrary predictor. Correlation can be high despite biased scale/offset and wide individual errors. A statistically detectable effect can be smaller than the benefit of simply asking a short questionnaire.

**Observed versus stated preferences:** logs reduce recall of event counts but reflect what a platform offered and what a schedule allowed. Self-report can reveal intention and cross-context experience but also contains desirability, recall, acquiescence and language effects. Neither is privileged “truth.” Likes are choices among exposures, not unconstrained preferences; sparse activity is not a stated dislike. Compare methods against external outcomes and each other rather than replacing self-report by fiat.

| Competing explanation | Falsifiable comparison required |
|---|---|
| Stable environment creates apparent person consistency | Compare context-only and person-history models; test after schedule changes and across dyads, with a separately consented standardized interaction study if causal attribution is essential. A person random intercept alone cannot resolve this. |
| Partner drives latency/starts | Hold partners/dyads out; record consented partner/context covariates; use a crossed design to separate actor/partner effects where identifiable. Nested one-partner data cannot identify both. |
| Platform creates the pattern | Matched-person and ideally matched-dyad periods; platform-specific exposure definitions; platform-held-out evaluation. Feature fusion and model-prediction agreement are insufficient. |
| User selects unusually salient chats | Enumerate consented eligible dyads before analysis; record selection/refusal counts; drop/reweight subsets and test deliberate truncation. Unknown omitted history remains unidentified. |
| Correlated measures manufacture confidence | Cluster by people/networks and time; do not multiply evidence from starts, restarts and volume or duplicate exports. Report joint performance against a simple baseline. |
| Circular validation | Never define “extravert” as high initiation and validate using initiation. Collect instruments independently before showing output; use future outcomes and independent raters, not app-generated labels. |

These hypotheses can coexist. Adjusting observed context cannot eliminate unmeasured confounding, and observational prediction never proves a psychological cause. Context-specific findings may be useful even if a universal personal factor fails.

## Methods suited to the question

Descriptive per-export counts, medians and explicit coverage remain the strongest method for reporting what occurred. For a few selected exports, a hierarchical model adds assumptions without identifying stable traits. It is not a sophistication upgrade.

With an adequately recruited longitudinal cohort, multilevel models can separate within-person time variation from between-person differences, shrink noisy estimates and represent dependent dyads. Partner/person/platform components need a crossed sampling design to be separately estimable. Person-centered longitudinal analyses can answer whether deviations from one's own pattern covary with an independent criterion; a cross-sectional between-person association cannot answer that.

Evaluate simple history/context baselines first. Use chronological splits for future observations, participant/network splits for new people, dyad splits for new relationships, and platform-held-out tests for new media. Fit transformations and select features inside training folds. Report raw errors, calibration and useful interval widths, not just correlation. Extra model complexity earns its place only through preregistered external improvement.

The prior [audit §28](../product/chatsense-full-project-audit.md#28-scientific-validation-program) uses strong “not inferable in principle” language for some inner-state claims. This review retains the **product prohibition** while distinguishing philosophical impossibility from an empirically unvalidated probabilistic association. A future consented study can examine subjective closeness; it cannot recover a particular partner's unobserved motive as fact. No current safety contract is relaxed.
