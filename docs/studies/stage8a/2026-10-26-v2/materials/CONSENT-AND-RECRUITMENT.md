# Neutral consent and recruitment kit — proposed, not sent

## Operational launch decisions (owner must complete before P1)

The P0 packet fixes methods; these operational facts are not silently assumed:
- Explicit dated approval to begin P1 on the frozen packet/commit.
- Named facilitator, two human first-pass reviewers (at least one independent of facilitation) and the human adjudication arrangement; identify recruitment/facilitation relationships and avoid coercion. Astra/Sol remain scientific/technical advisors on nonidentifying aggregate/code-only material.
- Approved private contact channel and encrypted, access-restricted storage location (outside Git/GitHub), with responsible data custodian. The owner supplies a reachable withdrawal/contact address before showing this sheet.
- Compensation choice fixed before contacting anyone: none or a stated small thank-you/voucher; no payment depends on completion or positive answers. No purchase is authorized now.
- Confirm local institutional/research requirements, if any, and that the chosen recruitment setting permits this small usability pilot. This packet makes no claim of ethics-board approval.
- Verify display/device against STIMULUS-REVIEW.md; schedule approximately 30 minutes without recording.
- Schedule sessions, both reviews and withdrawal-window closure within the stated per-session retention window. If a delay makes review impossible before deletion is due, delete on time and record the resulting missingness; do not extend retention or claim readiness for P2 from unavailable records.

Until these are supplied, do not distribute recruitment or consent wording. These are expected P1 launch decisions; distinguish them from any P0 scientific blocker.

## Invitation template (after authorization only)

"We are preparing a small study of how people understand screens from ChatSense, a tool that describes patterns in a chat export. The session takes about 30 minutes and uses five fictional examples. We are interested in your interpretation, including confusion or dislike; there are no right answers you need to give us and no effect on any relationship or service if you decline.

Adults aged 18 or older may volunteer. We will not ask to see your messages, personal chat exports, screenshots, contacts or identifying details about another person. You may skip questions or stop at any time. If interested, contact [owner-approved private study contact]. [Insert the fixed compensation statement.]"

Use an intermediary's opt-in invitation, not pressure from a developer's close friend. No mass outreach or messages are sent by P0. Keep scheduling/contact records separate from study responses. Do not collect exact birth date, employer, relationship partner identity or third-party consent.

## Eligibility and quotas (private, minimal)

Ask: "Are you 18 or older?" "Would you freely choose to take part?"
Optional: "Are you currently experiencing uncertainty about a romantic or friendship conversation?" yes/no/prefer not.
Optional: "For thinking about whether this tool might be useful to you, would you have a non-romantic conversation in mind?" yes/no/prefer not.
No follow-up about what happened. Quota categories can overlap. Screen broad category only if needed; do not require a disclosure of relationship details.

## Consent sheet — read before collecting responses

"This is a study of how adults understand five fictional ChatSense screens. You will read each screen, explain it in your own words, and rate aspects of it. It should take about 30 minutes. We are evaluating the screens, not you. Some fictional examples involve changes in communication and silence; you may find those topics uncomfortable.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question, take a break or stop without penalty. [Insert fixed compensation statement.] We do not need or want any of your messages, exports, personal screenshots, other people's names or identifying stories. Please keep your answers about the fictional examples. If a personal detail slips out, we will omit or remove it from the study notes.

We will take typed notes of your answers and ratings under a study code, with no audio/video recording. The facilitator can read back a note if you want to check its accuracy. With your agreement, two human reviewers and, if needed, a human adjudicator will read those notes to assess how the screens were understood. Your response notes and quotations will not be sent to an AI service under this protocol. Scientific and technical advisors may review nonidentifying aggregate results or code-only summaries. Your answers will not be used to judge your own conversations or other people.

Contact/scheduling details and this consent record are kept separately from response notes. Only the approved study team can access the private encrypted records. Raw notes and the code-to-contact link are deleted within 30 days after your session; scheduling/contact and individual consent records are deleted on the same schedule unless an applicable requirement is disclosed to you BEFORE consent. We will complete coding within that window. Aggregate, nonidentifying results and the methods may be retained indefinitely and shared in a public project report. If a required retention rule conflicts, the owner must amend this sheet before recruitment, not extend storage after your consent.

Until 14 days after your session, you can ask [owner-approved contact] to delete your notes using your study code. We will not publish human results until that withdrawal window closes. After deletion of the link and publication of aggregates, it may be impossible to locate your contribution. If you withdraw, we delete your response notes and the link between your contact details and study code. We retain only nonidentifying aggregate counts of sessions started, completed, withdrawn or excluded so that missing sessions are not hidden in the study results. These counts contain no study code, answers or contact details. This aggregate-count policy is part of the consent you can accept or decline before taking part. You can ask questions now or later at that contact."

Separate yes/no records:
1. I confirm I am 18+ and freely consent to the session, private typed notes, human review/adjudication and the disclosed aggregate-count policy. A no stops the session before any study data.
2. OPTIONAL: I agree to short, nonidentifying quotations in a public report. A no has no effect on participation.

Record verbal consent privately with date/time and study code; no name/signature is placed on the response form. Give the person their study code and contact/withdrawal details. Do not collect optional quote consent by default or infer it from session consent. No audio/video is planned; recording would need a separate amended consent/protocol.

## Incident handling and closure

If someone starts sharing a real conversation: "Please keep the details private. We can stay with the fictional example, skip this question, or stop." Do not transcribe the content, upload it to an AI tool, or inspect their device. Stop screen sharing if it reveals personal material. Remove accidental identifiers locally and record only that a redaction occurred. No personal export is needed even after the study.

At the end, offer the neutral debrief in INTERVIEW.md. Give no advice about the person's relationships. Distress ends the session at the person's choice. Do not market the product, encourage repeated checking or recruit a referral under pressure.
