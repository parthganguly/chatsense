# Private recording and independent review

The blank JSON and JSON Schema are form templates, not data collection software. Copy them only into the owner-approved encrypted study folder after P1 authorization. Never fill them in this Git checkout. Complete records, consent/contact files, quotes, evidence snippets and first-pass reviewer files stay off GitHub and are not sent to consulting models. Two humans conduct the first-pass review; a human resolves disagreements under the frozen rubric. Astra/Sol can review nonidentifying aggregate/code-only summaries, not response text. Any proposed AI processing of text needs separate owner authorization and an amended consent first. P0 authorizes synthetic controls only.

Use P01–P10 in first-exposure order. The schema permits nulls so a genuinely blank template validates; a valid JSON document is not automatically a complete, eligible study record. Before P2, manually verify consent, five unique A–E blocks, planned/actual order, exact stimulus hashes, timing/visibility/deviations, valid integer ratings, missing reasons, the three core verbatim questions on every card, and D's final next-event response. A/C/E and B have no next-event question: mark silence_next as not_applicable, not skipped. Codes must be restricted to the appropriate domain in CODING-RUBRIC.md; non-D history is not applicable. No rating is derived from text.

For every substantive code, store evidence_field, the safe exact phrase and a rationale. Keep two separate first-pass files; do not put one reviewer's answers in the other's copy. Mask ratings, wants, overall trust, screening and the other review. Each reviewer has a distinct ID, dated lock, and private SHA-256 of the locked file. Preserve originals when adjudicating. Complete the disagreement ledger even if the resolution remains AMBIGUOUS. Recheck any apparent contradiction across fields, especially a D reply ERROR versus a NO_PROMISE history code. Such contradictions must be resolved under the rubric; they may not be passed to aggregation as clean observations.

Only after labels are locked should the analysis reviewer receive ratings and eligibility flags. Independently recompute C/10, promise classifications, five usefulness medians, the ten within-person creepiness medians and their median, and the count of distinct people with at least one yes. Report gate criteria separately. The FAKE-only preflight calculator is a transparent arithmetic reference, not a human ingestion pipeline or proof that self-attested review flags are true.

Maintain a private disposition ledger for pre-exposure eligibility/declines, first exposure slots, withdrawal and exclusions. Preserve planned denominators using the minimal aggregate disposition counts disclosed in base consent. On withdrawal, delete content and the contact/code link; retain no linkable individual row. Anonymous arithmetic placeholders are not participant records. Public reporting uses aggregate counts only; do not publish individual rows, timestamps, IDs linked to quotes, rare context combinations or recruitment contact trails.

End-question missingness is descriptive. Core missingness, unresolved coding, consent/eligibility failures or material exposure contamination cannot produce PASS. Document all deviations before examining aggregate outcomes. Administrative correction of a transcription mistake requires its source/confirmation, date and reason; never quietly edit an answer to agree with the rubric.
