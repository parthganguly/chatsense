## Exact target

Prepare for **N=10 consenting adults** (existing #29 allows 8–10; prefer ten for the existing 8/10 acceptance rule), at least three currently navigating uncertain romantic/friendship contact and at least two considering non-romantic contact, without soliciting personal chat messages or identifying third-party details. Use only five **actual rendered synthetic scenario cards**: pattern change; steady/balanced; carried contact; unusual silence with restart history; not enough data. Ten seconds of silent reading, approximately four minutes per card, starting-card rotation, and the question order specified by #29.

Existing binding gates: at least **8/10** understand no motives or reply prediction; **zero** interpret the next-pattern sentence as a promise; median usefulness **>=4** on at least two of five cards; median creepiness **<=2**; at least half would want this for their own chat. Do not reinterpret thresholds after seeing data, imply representativeness from ten people, or treat 50 card ratings as 50 independent participants.
